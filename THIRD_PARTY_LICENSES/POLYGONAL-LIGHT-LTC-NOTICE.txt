Prime includes numeric GGX LTC fit tables from data/ggx_ltc_fit/fit0.dat through fit50.dat in
that supplemental archive. Each source slice contains a 64 by 64 grid; the slices span F0 from
zero through one. Prime converts the fitted matrices to the same normalized 16-bit UNORM
five-coefficient representation used by the reference renderer and stores the fitted directional
albedo beside it. The resulting resources are:

- prime/light/ggx_ltc_matrix.bytes.gz.b64
  decoded SHA-256: e2fc71977a4c35c32c5c26621875959e9504431e330f2993204ef4013b770fea
- prime/light/ggx_ltc_amplitude.bytes.gz.b64
  decoded SHA-256: c720db27d3cffee3eba898cce6009b9c9ce6a3d9337cd488c5c9c672b4b8b140

The supplemental archive does not state a separate software license for these numeric fit data.
This notice records their exact provenance and conversion; they are not represented as original
Prime data. Prime's LTC lookup, transform, proposal-density and state-packing source is an
independent implementation and remains covered by Prime's license.
