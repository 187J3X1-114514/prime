Prime's realtime spatiotemporal blue-noise lookup table was optimized with
FastNoise, Electronic Arts' F.A.S.T. Sampling implementation:

https://github.com/electronicarts/fastnoise

Prime generated the table with commit
2cf53e4bb510d07511fe63a312556d2a2e108c70 using Vector2/Uniform samples,
Gaussian sigma 1.0 spatial filtering, exponential alpha=beta=0.1 temporal
filtering, separate combination at spatial weight 0.5, and 10,000 iterations.
`tools/build_realtime_stbn.py` records the complete deterministic build and
verifies that optimization preserves the input digital nets.
